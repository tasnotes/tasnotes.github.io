old people. theyre odd. some weird but most...
just alive(?) maybe half sentient. i dont
interact with them much. worked at a gym once, 
naked ol' men walking around cheeks flapping and
small talk- mostly effortful breathing. kinda cute
those things, diligent with gifts. they might 
have a hole or two plus a stain but its free underwear(the best of gifts)!

old man Ta. hmm i cant imagine myself an old 
person. there's one at work, a loud man with little 
shame, two ex wives but entertaining. one of those
hippies im guessing from decades back. ah that reminds
me of a vietnam veteran that frequented when i 
worked at a corner store. he was homeless but i
tried to help him however i could. was shut down
when i questioned about the war, stupid me too caught
up in my history assignment but thats little excuse. 
really loved his daughter it seems. sent her mail 
and little gifts, one a small tiger balm he was eyeing 
for some time. he paid for hers but i slid 
a can for himself. never drank which was odd but
not unexpected. he had dogs to feed afterall, waddling
with little coats and stood sentry near the door
with every visit. ah this was a few winters ago
having long since quit. he was beaming with her reply, though i wonder if she knew. hope he's doing alright. 

i dont want to grow old. maybe that'd change if
i had a wife and kids and grandkids but thats..to be seen haha.
though if i was an old man, id be a cool old man at 
the very least, certainly not leaving gifts for the gym staff, but thats somewhat cool too.
